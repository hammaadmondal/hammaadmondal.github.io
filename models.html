Models

Every model is built to be audited in about ten minutes.

Each initiation ships with its Excel model. The page for each model summarises the key assumptions so you can see what drives the target before you download.

Model standard

Tabs

Assumptions, income statement, balance sheet, cash flow, DCF, comps and scenarios, plus a checks tab.

Rules

Inputs are colour-coded and kept on the assumptions tab. No hard-coded numbers sit inside formulas. The checks tab confirms the balance sheet balances and cash ties.
