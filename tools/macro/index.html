Reading the dashboard

How this feeds a valuation

Risk-free rate

WACC starts here

The cost of equity is the risk-free rate plus beta times the equity risk premium. A 10-year AAA or Bund yield is the usual euro starting point, so a 50bp move in yields moves every DCF on this site.

Euribor

Bank earnings

Irish banks earn the gap between what loans and deposits pay. Most of that tracks Euribor and the ECB deposit rate, which is why the AIB note leans on the rate path.

Curve shape

Growth signal

When short rates sit above long rates (an inverted curve), markets expect cuts and slower growth. A steepening curve usually means the opposite.
