Disclosures

Student work, not investment advice.

Everything on this site is an educational exercise. Please read this page before relying on anything here.

Important information

  • Not advice. The content is not investment, financial, legal or tax advice, and is not an offer or solicitation to buy or sell any security. The author is a student and is not a licensed or regulated investment adviser.
  • Illustrative views. Ratings, price targets and forecasts are the author's own illustrative opinions and may be wrong. Do not use them to make investment decisions.
  • Rating definitions. Ratings compare the 12-month price target with the share price on the price date. Overweight: upside of 15% or more. Neutral: between -10% and +15%. Underweight: downside of 10% or more.
  • Data. Published figures come from company filings, results releases and public market data, with the source and date under each table and chart. The models were built with the help of licensed market data. Raw licensed data is never published: peer multiples drawn from consensus estimates are shown only as derived medians and ranges.
  • How the work was prepared. Research, modelling and drafting were done with AI assistance (Claude, by Anthropic). The author reviewed the figures, owns the views and can explain every assumption. No text or charts are copied from broker research.
  • Dated views. Every report carries a publication date and a price date. A price target older than 12 months is historical and not current.
  • Positions. At publication the author holds no position (shares, CFDs, options or other derivatives) in any company covered: AIB Group plc and Ryanair Holdings plc. Each report repeats its own position statement, and this page will be updated if that changes.
  • No compensation or conflicts. The author receives no payment from any company covered, and is not employed by, interning at, or advising any company covered.
  • Accuracy. Care is taken, but figures may contain errors. Please check against company filings.
