Method

How the research is built

01

Sourced

Figures come from filings, company releases and public market data. Every table and chart names its source and date.

02

Modelled

Each initiation has a downloadable Excel model with tabs for assumptions, statements, valuation, comps and scenarios, plus a checks tab.

03

Dated

Every note carries a publication and price date. Once a target is more than 12 months old it reads as historical.
