How the research is built

Sourced

Figures come from filings, company releases and public market data. Each chart names its source and date.

Modelled

Each initiation has a downloadable Excel model with separate tabs for assumptions, statements, DCF, comps and scenarios, plus a checks tab.

Dated

Every note is date-stamped. Once a price target is older than 12 months it reads as historical, not current.

What each initiation contains

  • Rating, 12-month price target, current price and upside.
  • A three-point thesis and a section on what the market is pricing in.
  • Key financials: two actual years and three forecast years.
  • A valuation section with a football-field chart, blending DCF and peer multiples.
  • Bull, base and bear scenarios, risks, and a WACC versus terminal growth sensitivity table.
