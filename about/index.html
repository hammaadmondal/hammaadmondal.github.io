About

Hammaad Mondal

MSc Finance student at UCD Michael Smurfit Graduate Business School and CFA Level I candidate, working towards a career in equity research.

I'm based in Dublin, studying for an MSc in Finance at UCD Smurfit. My coursework in Equity Asset Valuation, Capital Markets and Instruments, and Financial Econometrics is the core of what I want to do: understand a business, model it, and decide what it is worth.

This site is where I publish that work. Each initiation starts with a question the market is debating, tests it against the company's filings, and ends with a model, a valuation and a rating I can defend. The first two cover Ryanair and AIB Group.

Before Dublin, I spent four months in Audit & Assurance at EY Abu Dhabi, working on engagements for a $350B+ AUM state asset manager and a master developer. Tracing figures back to source documents, and chasing unreconciled differences until they were explained, showed me how much a single unsupported number can undermine a balance. I hold every figure in a model to the same standard.

I first built a full valuation through the CFA Research Challenge, where I modelled Hostelworld Group and presented a Buy recommendation. On the quantitative side I work in Python (Pandas, NumPy, SciPy), and recently built a probability and cross-market arbitrage engine that processes live market price feeds.

I graduated cum laude with a BBA in Accounting from Texas Tech University, completed in three years. I'm open to equity research, investment analysis and asset management roles in Dublin, London and the UAE.
